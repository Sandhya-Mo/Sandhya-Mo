I study Computer Science and Physics at Northeastern, with a minor in Mechanical Engineering. Most of my time goes into building satellites.

Right now I'm the flight software lead and command and data handling lead for TeraLink 1, a 6U CubeSat that will be the first to demonstrate sub THz communication to and from low Earth orbit. I write our F Prime components in C++, run our flatsat testing, and maintain the command and telemetry database. I'm also the software lead and simulations deputy at the Northeastern Satellite Laboratory, where I started our business team.

I'm also working on DEWSat, a drought detection CubeSat that made it to the finals of NASA's Space to Soil Challenge and will be presented at AIAA SciTech 2027. I'm also leading a Northeastern PEAK Fellowship project designing an open source deployable solar array for 3U CubeSats.

I mostly work in Python, C++, MATLAB, and SolidWorks.

I'm looking for a coop from January to June 2027. You can reach me at moorthi.sa@northeastern.edu or on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sandhya--moorthi).
